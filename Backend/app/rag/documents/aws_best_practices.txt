AWS EC2 instances with average CPU utilization below 10% over a 7-day period are considered idle and should be stopped to eliminate unnecessary cost.

Instances with CPU utilization between 10% and 20% are over-provisioned and should be downgraded to a smaller instance type to reduce monthly spend.

Cost anomalies are detected using Z-score analysis. A Z-score above 2.0 indicates a statistically significant deviation from historical spending and should trigger an alert.

Stopping an idle EC2 instance is generally safe if there are no active network connections and the instance has been idle for more than 24 hours.

Downgrading an instance type requires a stop and restart, causing a brief downtime of typically under 60 seconds. Schedule this during off-peak hours to minimize impact on users.
